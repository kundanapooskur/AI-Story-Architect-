## ✨ Story Generator Examples

### Example 1: Dark Fantasy with Image + Audio

**Configuration:**
- Genre: Fantasy
- Writing Style: Descriptive and vivid
- POV: First Person
- Tone: Dark
- Story Length: 1000 words
- ✅ Generate Scene Image
- ✅ Generate Audio Narration

**Story Idea:**
```
I stand at the entrance of the Obsidian Citadel at midnight, my silver blade drawn as dark storm clouds gather overhead. The fortress of black volcanic glass towers above me, its windows glowing with sickly green light. My wolf companion growls at my side as shadowy figures emerge from the gates - the corrupted guards I once called friends. I have one chance to stop the dark ritual before the blood moon rises.
```

## 👤 Character Creator Examples

### Example 1: Fantasy Assassin

**Name:** Kira Shadowblade

**Personality:**
```
Cold and calculating on the surface, but fiercely protective of innocents. Struggles with the morality of her profession. Uses dark humor to cope with guilt. Trust issues but desperately wants connection.
```

**Backstory:**
```
Orphaned at age 6 when her merchant parents were murdered by a rival guild. Raised by the Shadow Syndicate, trained as an assassin from childhood. At 25, discovered her parents were killed on false charges - her entire life built on a lie. Now works independently, choosing her own targets. Hunts those who prey on the weak while grappling with becoming what she hates.
```

**Speech Style:**
```
Clipped and efficient. Uses assassin jargon. Sarcastic when nervous. Rarely uses contractions when serious. Whispers when threatening.
```

**Goals:**
```
Expose the Shadow Syndicate's corruption, Find her parents' real killer, Protect street orphans, Retire from killing
```

**Fears:**
```
Becoming a monster, Being betrayed again, Caring about someone who dies, Losing her skills with age
```

## 📋 Plot Outliner Examples

### Example 1: Mystery Thriller

**Act 1 - Setup:**

**Opening:**
```
Detective Sarah Chen finds a coded letter in her dead father's safe deposit box - a father she thought died of natural causes 10 years ago. The code matches an unsolved serial killer case from the 1990s.
```

**Inciting Incident:**
```
Sarah receives a package containing her childhood doll with a fresh photo of her daughter at school inside. A note reads: "Your father couldn't solve me. Can you? The game begins again."
```

**Act 2 - Confrontation:**

**Rising Action:**
```
Sarah investigates secretly, unable to trust her department. Each clue leads to more questions. She discovers her father had a secret investigation - five cold cases, all daughters of detectives. The pattern is clear: the killer targets detective families across generations.
```

**Midpoint:**
```
Sarah finds video footage from her father's last night alive. He's meeting someone in a police uniform. She recognizes the voice - it's her current partner, Detective Marcus Webb. The man she's worked with for 8 years. The killer has been beside her the whole time.
```

**Crisis:**
```
Sarah's daughter is kidnapped. Marcus gives her 24 hours to solve the puzzle her father couldn't. If she solves it, her daughter lives. If she fails, the girl dies. Sarah must work alone, racing against time.
```

**Act 3 - Resolution:**

**Climax:**
```
Sarah solves the puzzle and confronts Marcus at an abandoned amusement park. Marcus reveals his daughter was the first victim in 1985, killed by a detective's negligence. He's been punishing detective families ever since. Sarah's father got close, so Marcus killed him. The confrontation is psychological - Sarah must convince Marcus his daughter wouldn't want this legacy of pain.
```

**Resolution:**
```
Marcus surrenders. Sarah's daughter is safe but traumatized. Sarah retires to focus on family. The case closes but scars remain. She plants a tree in her father's memory, teaching her daughter that justice sometimes comes with impossible costs.
```

### Example 2: Fantasy Epic

**Act 1 - Setup:**

**Opening:**
```
Kael Stormforge lives as a blacksmith, hiding his royal blood. He's the exiled prince, hunted for fifteen years. Life is simple until a stranger arrives with a sword bearing the royal crest, delivers a message: "The king is poisoned. The kingdom falls. Only true heir can wield the Crown of Flames. Seven days."
```

**Inciting Incident:**
```
That night, twenty assassins attack. Kael fights them off using combat skills he's hidden for years. The village sees. His secret is out. An old woman gives him a map: "The Crown is in the Fire Mountains. Your father hid it before he died. Save the kingdom or let it burn."
```

**Act 2 - Confrontation:**

**Rising Action:**
```
Kael journeys to Fire Mountains, gathering allies: Zara the bandit queen, Finn the exiled mage, Ash the war-golem. Each trial tests them - Bridge of Mirrors shows worst fears, Canyon of Echoes forces them to face past betrayals, Flame Trials burn away lies.
```

**Midpoint:**
```
Kael reaches the Crown's chamber and discovers the truth: his father didn't hide it. He destroyed it. The Crown is actually a curse that burns wielder's humanity, transforming them into a fire elemental of pure destruction. His uncle knows this - wants Kael to claim it and become a monster, proving the royal line corrupt.
```

**Crisis:**
```
Uncle attacks with an army while Kael is trapped in mountains. His allies are captured. The kingdom burns. Kael must choose: claim the Crown and become a monster to save his people, or refuse and watch everything die.
```

**Act 3 - Resolution:**

**Climax:**
```
Kael doesn't claim the Crown. He shatters it completely. The power releases as pure flame, but Kael channels it through his allies' magic and his blacksmith skills. He forges a new artifact - the Shield of Embers - that protects rather than destroys. His uncle arrives expecting a monster. Finds a king. They duel. Kael wins through purpose, not power.
```

**Resolution:**
```
Kael takes the throne but establishes a council including commoners, mages, and former enemies. He keeps his blacksmith forge, spending one day a week making tools for farmers. The Crown is gone, but something better remains: a kingdom built on choice rather than birthright.
```

## 🔬 Synthetic Data Examples

### Example 1: Alternative Story Branches

**Scene to Vary:**
```
The detective finally corners the serial killer in the abandoned amusement park. Rusted carousel horses spin slowly in the wind. The killer holds a detonator. 'You can save the hostages or chase me,' he says. 'But not both. Ten seconds to decide.' The detective's hand hovers over their gun.
```

**Number of Variations:** 5

**Expected Output Types:**
1. Heroic choice (saves hostages, killer escapes)
2. Tragic choice (catches killer, hostages die)
3. Failed attempt (tries both, fails at both)
4. Clever twist (discovers hostages fake, shoots killer)
5. Peaceful resolution (talks killer down)
